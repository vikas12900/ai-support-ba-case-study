# NorthPeak Bank – Customer Support Knowledge Base

FICTIONAL BANK, created for a portfolio demo. All policies, fees and limits below are invented.
Each section has an ID. The assistant must cite the IDs it used.

---

## KB-00 About this assistant (effective 1 Jan 2026)
- Customers are talking to an AI assistant, not a person. A human agent is available 24/7 through in-app chat.
- NorthPeak will never ask for a full card number, CVV, PIN, app passcode or one-time code in chat.
- The assistant cannot see accounts, balances, transactions or card status. It cannot issue refunds, cancel transfers or change personal details. These go to a human agent.

## KB-01 Card delivery (effective 1 Jan 2026)
- Standard delivery to a UK address is free and takes up to 5 working days from the day the card is ordered.
- Delivery to an international address takes up to 10 working days.
- Express delivery (UK addresses only) costs £5 and arrives in 1–2 working days. Express delivery includes a courier tracking number.
- Standard delivery has no courier tracking. The app shows the card status under Cards: "Ordered", then "Dispatched".
- If the card has not arrived after 10 working days (UK) or 15 working days (international), report it in the app under Cards > Card not received. We cancel that card and send a new one free of charge.
- The delivery address can only be changed before the status shows "Dispatched".

## KB-02 Activating and linking your card (effective 1 Jan 2026)
- New cards arrive inactive for security.
- To activate: open the app, go to Cards > Activate card, and enter the last 4 digits and the expiry date printed on the card.
- Activating a card also links it to your account. There is no separate linking step.
- Make the first payment with chip and PIN. Contactless works after that first chip and PIN payment.
- If activation fails three times, the customer needs a human agent.

## KB-03 Extra, virtual and disposable cards (effective 1 Jan 2026)
- The first physical card is free.
- Up to 3 physical cards can be active at once. Each extra physical card costs £5. Order in the app under Cards > Order card.
- Virtual cards are free, created instantly in the app (Cards > New virtual card), up to 5 at a time. They work for online payments and with Apple Pay and Google Pay.
- Disposable virtual cards get a new card number automatically after each payment, which protects against online card fraud.
- Disposable card limits: up to 5 disposable cards can be created per day, and each payment can be up to £1,000.
- Disposable cards cannot be used for subscriptions or recurring payments, or for ATM withdrawals.

## KB-04 Card expiry (effective 1 Jan 2026)
- A replacement card is sent automatically, free of charge, 30 days before the current card expires.
- The old card keeps working until the end of the month printed on it.

## KB-05 Where and how your card works (effective 1 Jan 2026)
- All NorthPeak cards are Visa debit cards. NorthPeak does not issue Mastercard.
- Cards work wherever Visa is accepted, in over 150 countries. A few merchants, such as some car rental firms, may not accept debit cards.
- Customers can hold and exchange GBP, EUR, USD and 25 other currencies in the app.
- Apple Pay and Google Pay are supported. Add the card in the app under Cards > Add to Apple Wallet or Cards > Add to Google Pay.
- The contactless limit is £100 per payment.
- If contactless does not work: make sure the first payment on a new card was chip and PIN, and check contactless is switched on under Cards > Security. Card terminals sometimes ask for chip and PIN for security. In that case, insert the card.

## KB-06 PIN (effective 1 Jan 2026)
- View the PIN in the app under Cards > Show PIN.
- The PIN can only be changed at a UK ATM that offers PIN services. It cannot be changed in the app.
- Entering the wrong PIN 3 times blocks it. Unblock it in the app under Cards > Unblock PIN, then make the next payment with chip and PIN.

## KB-07 ATM withdrawals (effective 1 March 2026)
- Cash can be withdrawn at any ATM showing the Visa logo, worldwide.
- Withdrawals are free up to £200 per calendar month. Above that, the fee is 2% of the amount (minimum £1 per withdrawal).
- Some ATM operators add their own fee. It is shown on the ATM screen before confirming. NorthPeak does not control or refund operator fees.
- The daily cash withdrawal limit is £500.
- If an ATM keeps the card or gives the wrong amount of cash, the customer must speak to a human agent.

## KB-08 Fees and charges (effective 1 March 2026 – CURRENT)
- Card payments in GBP: free.
- Card payments in a foreign currency: Visa exchange rate. No fee Monday to Friday. A 0.5% markup applies on Saturdays and Sundays.
- ATM withdrawals: see KB-07.
- Transfers within the UK: free.
- International transfers: £3 per transfer, plus an exchange fee that is shown before the customer confirms.
- Currency exchange in the app: free up to £1,000 per calendar month, then 0.5%. There are no discounts for frequent exchanges.
- Top-up with a UK debit card: free. Top-up with a credit card: 1.5%. Top-up with a card issued outside the UK, including European cards: 1%.
- Top-up by bank transfer: NorthPeak does not charge. The sending bank may charge its own fee, especially for international transfers.

- Currency exchange in the app free up to £2,000 per month.

## KB-09 Topping up (effective 1 Jan 2026)
- Ways to top up: debit or credit card in the app (instant), Apple Pay or Google Pay in the app (instant), or bank transfer to your NorthPeak sort code and account number (usually within 2 hours, up to 1 working day).
- Cash and cheque top-ups are not accepted.
- Limits: maximum account balance £40,000. Each card top-up must be between £10 and £5,000. Card top-ups are limited to £15,000 per rolling 30 days. Bank transfers have no separate limit, apart from the maximum balance.
- Auto top-up: switch it on under Accounts > Auto top-up. Choose a threshold (for example £20) and an amount. When the balance drops below the threshold, the saved card is charged automatically. It can be switched off at any time.
- Verifying a top-up card: the first time a new card is used to top up, NorthPeak may make a small temporary charge (under £2) with a 4-digit code in the statement description. Enter that code in the app under Top up > Verify card within 7 days. The small charge is refunded automatically.
- If a top-up is pending, failed or reversed, the customer needs a human agent to check it.

## KB-10 Freezing a card (effective 1 Jan 2026)
- A card can be frozen instantly in the app under Cards > Freeze card. This blocks all new payments and withdrawals. It can be unfrozen at any time.
- If a card is lost or stolen, or there is a payment the customer does not recognise, they should freeze the card straight away and speak to a human agent.
